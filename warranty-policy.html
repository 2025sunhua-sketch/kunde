Warranty & After-sales Policy

Our commitment to quality and customer support

Last updated: September 2026

Thank you for choosing KDELEC. As a B2B manufacturer and supplier of electrical and electronic products, we are committed to providing reliable products and professional after-sales support to our business partners.

1. Product Warranty

All KDELEC products are manufactured under strict quality control and come with a standard warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. The specific warranty period and terms are subject to the quotation, contract, or order confirmation agreed upon between KDELEC and the customer.

2. Quality Assurance

Every product is inspected before shipment to ensure it meets the agreed specifications. We welcome customers to arrange third-party inspection prior to delivery if required.

3. Returns & Replacements

As a B2B supplier, we do not operate a standard online return process. Return or replacement requests are handled on a case-by-case basis according to the terms of the relevant order:

  • If a product is found to be defective or damaged upon arrival, please contact our sales team within 7 days of receipt with photos or videos as evidence.
  • Upon verification, we will arrange repair, replacement, or other appropriate solutions in accordance with the agreed terms.
  • Returns due to customer ordering errors (wrong model, specification, or quantity) are subject to prior approval and may incur restocking or shipping fees.

4. Technical Support

Our engineering team provides technical consultation, installation guidance, and troubleshooting support for all products we supply. Please contact us with the product model and a description of the issue.

5. Shipping & Lead Time

Shipping methods, lead times, and freight responsibilities are confirmed during the order process and stated in the proforma invoice or sales contract.

6. Contact Us

For any warranty, return, or after-sales inquiry, please contact our sales team:

Email: sales@kdelec.com
Website: https://kdelec.com

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
