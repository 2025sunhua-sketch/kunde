Frequently asked questions

What this catalogue contains, how to request a quotation, and what is deliberately not published.

  1. Home
  2. FAQ
Where is KUNDE ELECTRIC based?
Our office and showroom are at A0-033~035, Binwang Market, Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. Visits can be arranged in advance — contact us to agree a time.
What does KUNDE ELECTRIC do?
We are a Yiwu-based sourcing and quality-control partner for electrical connection and power-line hardware. We work with partner factories in the Yiwu power-fittings cluster and help overseas buyers identify, specify and consolidate products.
How large is the product catalogue?
This site lists 1,511 products across 39 browse categories, with 8,019 product photographs. Every listing links to a dedicated page with a gallery and, where available, supplier detail images.
Do you publish prices or minimum order quantities?
No. Prices, MOQs and lead times are not published on this site. Every item is quoted per enquiry, based on specification, quantity and destination. Request a written quotation before making a purchasing decision.
Where can I find dimensions and technical specifications?
Where the supplier supplied dimension tables, they are reproduced as photographs inside the product detail images on each listing. Machine-readable datasheets and tolerance drawings are available on request — tell us the product ID shown on the page.
Are the product descriptions verified technical data?
Product titles, images and detail graphics are reproduced as supplied by the source listing. They are a sourcing reference, not a certified datasheet. Confirm material, standard, plating and dimensions in writing before ordering.
Do you publish certification claims?
We do not publish blanket certification claims on this site. If your project requires ISO, IEC, CE, UL or other documentation, request the specific certificate for the specific product and we will confirm what can be supplied in writing.
Can I request samples, catalogues or a consolidated quotation?
Yes. Send the product IDs you are interested in, together with quantities and destination. PDF technical catalogues for the previous product range are available on the Resources page for reference.
What languages and business hours do you support?
The website is published in English only. Office hours are Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 and Saturday 09:00–12:00, China Standard Time (UTC+8).
What are the payment and delivery terms?
Terms are agreed per order and stated in the written quotation. We do not publish standard payment terms, lead times or shipping promises on this website.
How do I get a quote quickly?
Use the Request a Quote button on any product page — it pre-fills the product ID and title — or email sales@kdelec.com with the product IDs, required quantities and destination. WhatsApp and WeChat are both available on +86-13566954989.
Can you source a part that is not listed?
Send a photograph, drawing, model number or sample description. We will confirm whether an equivalent is available through our partner factories and reply with options in writing.
Why did the old product pages disappear?
The previous 322-page product section has been retired. It has been replaced by a new 1,511-item catalogue built from the current source listings. Old product URLs redirect to the new catalogue.
Do you have a physical showroom?
Yes — the showroom and office at Binwang Market, Yiwu can be visited. Please contact us in advance so the relevant samples can be prepared.
Editorial note. The previous site published 18 FAQ answers containing figures and commercial promises that could not be substantiated (factory size, headcount, years of experience, countries served, ISO certification, standard lead times and payment terms). Those items were not migrated. The answers above describe what this website actually publishes.
