What specific materials are used in your cable lugs and terminals?

Our bimetallic cable lugs are crafted using high-purity T2 copper and aluminum, joined via advanced friction welding technology. The copper contact surface is finished with a professional tin-plating to prevent oxidation, ensuring optimal conductivity and long-term reliability.

What international certifications and standards do your products comply with?

Our manufacturing system is certified under ISO 9001:2015. Our products are designed and tested with reference to key international standards, including:

  • IEC - International Electrotechnical Commission
  • UL - Underwriters Laboratories
  • CE - European Conformity
  • ANSI - American National Standards Institute
  • DIN - German Institute for Standardization

This ensures our products meet the diverse requirements of global power grids.

How do you package heavy electrical goods to ensure safe sea freight?

We use robust packaging protocols:

  • Products are first placed in thickened inner boxes
  • Then secured in reinforced outer crates (wooden or double-wall corrugated)
  • Sensitive copper components are additionally sealed in moisture-proof vacuum bags to prevent corrosion during transit
