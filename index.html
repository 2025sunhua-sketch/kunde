Full FAQ
Where is KUNDE ELECTRIC based?
Our office and showroom are at A0-033~035, Binwang Market, Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. Visits can be arranged in advance — contact us to agree a time.
What does KUNDE ELECTRIC do?
We are a Yiwu-based sourcing and quality-control partner for electrical connection and power-line hardware. We work with partner factories in the Yiwu power-fittings cluster and help overseas buyers identify, specify and consolidate products.
How large is the product catalogue?
This site lists 1,511 products across 39 browse categories, with 8,019 product photographs. Every listing links to a dedicated page with a gallery and, where available, supplier detail images.
Do you publish prices or minimum order quantities?
No. Prices, MOQs and lead times are not published on this site. Every item is quoted per enquiry, based on specification, quantity and destination. Request a written quotation before making a purchasing decision.
Where can I find dimensions and technical specifications?
Where the supplier supplied dimension tables, they are reproduced as photographs inside the product detail images on each listing. Machine-readable datasheets and tolerance drawings are available on request — tell us the product ID shown on the page.
