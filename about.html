About KUNDE ELECTRIC

A Yiwu-based sourcing and quality-control partner for electrical connection products and power-line hardware.

  1. Home
  2. About us

Based in Yiwu since 2015

Yiwu Kunde Electric Co., Ltd. was established in 2015 in Yiwu, Zhejiang, China. We work as a sourcing and quality-control partner for overseas buyers of electrical connection products and power-line hardware, cooperating with partner factories in the Yiwu power-fittings cluster.

What we do

We help buyers identify the right part, consolidate mixed product ranges, review supplier documentation and prepare written quotations. Our catalogue is published in English only and covers cable lugs, terminals, clamps, insulators, fuses, switches, control devices and associated line hardware.

Where our customers are

The previous site described exports to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa and South America. We continue to serve enquiries from those regions and elsewhere; specific shipment destinations are confirmed per order.

Our stated philosophy

"Credibility as the Cornerstone, Quality as the Foundation" is the principle the company has published since the previous site. On this website we apply it by labelling what is verified, what is reproduced as supplied, and what must be confirmed in writing.

What this website does not publish. Prices, MOQs, lead times, employee counts, factory floor area, number of countries served, and blanket ISO / IEC / CE / UL certification claims are deliberately absent. The previous site made a number of such claims that could not be substantiated; they have not been carried over. Ask us for written confirmation of anything your project requires.
